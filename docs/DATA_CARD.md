# DATA_CARD

## Provenance: real vs synthetic

| Data | Origin | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Personas (profiles, case studies, proposals, reviews), policy pack, mock marketplace (`jobs.json`, `clients.json`), labelled dev set | **SYNTHETIC**, hand-written and generated by `data/generate_mock.py`. The supervisor did not supply GigSim or personas. | `data/personas`, `data/policy_pack`, `data/mock_market` |
| Public job postings (optional, real) | Kaggle datasets below, ingested by `data/ingest_kaggle.py` | `data/processed/real_jobs.json` (not committed) |

Real records carry `"source": "kaggle:<owner/slug>"`. Synthetic records carry no such field (or `"source": "synthetic"`). Never mix them in evals without filtering on this field.
Freelancer portfolios, client histories, reviews, proposals and conversations do not exist in the real datasets. They stay synthetic.

## Attribution
ODC-By requires credit to the author (asaniczka) and a link to the license. Apache-2.0 requires keeping the license notice. Cite each dataset by its Kaggle URL `https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/<owner/slug>` in any report.

## Preprocessing (`data/ingest_kaggle.py`)
1. Map the posting's title, description and budget columns to the `jobs.json` schema.
2. Drop rows with an empty title or description. Drop exact duplicates on the lowercased title+description.
3. Keep only budgets above 0. Hourly-only postings get no `budget_usd` and are skipped.
4. Strip HTML tags and collapse whitespace.
5. Tag each record with its `source`.

Raw CSVs live in `data/raw/` and are git-ignored.

## Limitations
- Postings are Upwork-only, mostly 2023-2024, and reflect scraped listings, not outcomes. No proposals, bids or win/loss labels.
- Budgets are self-declared. Currency is assumed USD.
- Scraped client names or contact text may appear in descriptions. Do not publish raw rows.
- English-heavy. Non-English postings are not filtered.
- Synthetic personas are tiny (3 personas, 42 jobs) and hand-tuned. Metrics on them are not evidence of real-world performance.
- Fake-job labels (trial-1) come from a different platform than Upwork, so the distribution differs.

## Real vs synthetic, by component
**Real:** job text, budgets, client spend, verification and ratings, Connects costs, market percentiles.
**Synthetic:** personas and portfolios, the labelled dev set (gold actions, evidence, fair-price bands), planted scam and injection posts, client replies, and **all GigSim outcomes** (`outcome()` is a hand-written model: reply probability from requirement overlap, specific opening, length, price vs budget, client quality). GigSim reply rates therefore measure agreement with that model, not real-world success.
