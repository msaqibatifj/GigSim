# Legal-doc RAG for a law firm

Built retrieval-augmented question answering over 40k contract PDFs using BM25 plus dense vectors fused with RRF and a bge cross-encoder reranker. Recall@5 rose from 0.61 to 0.84. Stack: Python, Qdrant, FastAPI. 6 weeks.
